# Q1 / Q2 真题题干句式库

来源：2016–2024 年英语四级听力 News Report 全部真题，224 题逐题归类后提取。
用法：**优先套用真题原型改写**（替换人名、地名、机构名、事件），不要自造句式。
改写时保留句式骨架，只替换实词。

---

## 何事 · W1 事件经过型

真题原型（Q1）：
- What happened at a construction site in Brooklyn on Tuesday?
- What happened in New York's Prospect Park on Tuesday?
- What event will take place in Glasgow?

真题原型（Q2）：
- What happened in Scotland last Wednesday?
- What finally happened to the old man?

通用模板：
- `What happened to/on/in + 地点或人物 + 时间状语?`
- `What finally happened to + 人物/事物?`
- `What will happen + when/if + 条件?`
- `What event will take place in + 地点?`

要点：答案是一个**已发生或即将发生的事件**，干扰项常为把事件的地点、时间、主体对调。

---

## 何事 · W2 信息获知型

真题原型（Q1）：
- What is the news report mainly about?
- What is reported in the news?
- What do we learn about the new study from the news report?
- What do we learn from the news report about the study?
- What do we learn from the police report?

真题原型（Q2）：
- What do we learn from the report about tourism in France?
- What do we learn about the cow from the end of the news report?

通用模板：
- `What is the news report mainly about?`（主旨题，答案应是对全篇的概括）
- `What do we learn from the news report about + X?`
- `What do we learn about + X?`
- `What is reported in the news?`

要点：W2 的正确项往往是**概括或推断**，不是原文某一句的直接复述。干扰项常为「原文提到但只是细节、以偏概全」。

---

## 何事 · W3 行为举措型（占比最高）

真题原型（Q1）：
- What are scientists planning to do?
- What were people in parts of Scotland advised to do?
- What did Joe's parents decide to do?
- What did the old age pensioner plan to do?
- What is the finding of the AAA survey?
- What problem is Britain facing?
- What was the woman's problem?

真题原型（Q2）：
- What does the report say about the snake?
- What did the head of road policing urge drivers to do?
- What did the restaurant "Number 4" do?
- What is the scientists' recommendation?
- What does Glasgow City Council say the event will do?
- What does the report say was still unclear?
- What did the federal government do last month?
- What did city officials of Fayetteville want bus drivers to be aware of?

通用模板：
- `What did + 主体 + do/decide/attempt/try to do?`
- `What is/are + 主体 + planning/trying/aiming/hoping to do?`
- `What were + 群体 + advised/required/urged/expected to do?`
- `What does the news report say about + X?`
- `What is the finding/problem/result/recommendation of + X?`
- `What does + 机构 + say + 事物 + will do?`

要点：答案是一个**动作、举措、计划或研究结论**。干扰项常为「主体错位」（把 A 的计划安到 B 头上）或「程度偏移」（计划说成已完成）。

---

## 何因（Why / cause / purpose）

真题原型（Q1）：
- Why did John Gordon move out of Abergeldie Castle?
- Why does New York State want to turn off Niagara Falls?
- According to Google, what was the cause of the accident?
- What accounts most for the huge declines in tourism in France?
- What has caused the thick clouds in the photos taken by NASA satellite?
- Why did the police officer stop the SUV car?
- Why are families across the U.S. looking to this winter with fear?

真题原型（Q2）：
- Why did Paula Pierce give Clint Buffington the reward?
- Why did scientists choose to set up a 4G network in their mission?
- What is the cause of the problem according to the UK Health Secretary?

通用模板：
- `Why did/does + 主体 + 动作?`
- `What is/was the cause of + 事件 (+ according to + 来源)?`
- `What caused / What has caused + 现象?`
- `What accounts for + 现象?`
- `What is the purpose of + 事物?` / `For what purpose did + 主体 + 动作?`
- `What motivated + 人物 + to + 动作?`

要点：答案必须是**原文明确给出的原因**；若原文只给结果未给原因，则不能出此类题。干扰项常为「结果当原因」。

---

## 何人（Who / 某人说了什么 / 关于某人的信息）

真题原型（Q1）：
- What does the news report say about the waiter?
- What do we learn about Sherry Christian?
- What do we learn from the report about the 55-year-old woman?

真题原型（Q2）：
- What does Juan Somavia, Director-General of the International Labour Organization say?
- What did the spokesperson for United Airlines say they would do after the incident?
- What did the researchers acknowledge?
- What did the boy tell the police officers he was planning to do?
- What do NASA officials think about the thick clouds?
- What did the director of the city administration say at the end of the news report?

通用模板：
- `What does/did + 人物（常带职务同位语）+ say (about + X)?`
- `What do we learn/know about + 人物 (+ from the news report)?`
- `What does the news report say about + 人物?`
- `Who + 谓语动词 + ...?`

要点：Q2 的「何人」高频形态是**转述某人的话或观点**，正确项常含 `say / think / acknowledge / believe` 的 paraphrase。干扰项常为「把甲的观点安给乙」。

---

## 如何（How）—— 本技能中极少出现，备用

真题原型：
- How did Kelly Swisher feel when she first came across the rat snake?
- How was the woman's problem eventually solved?
- How have Google's self-driving cars performed so far?

通用模板：
- `How did + 主体 + 动作?`
- `How was + 事件 + solved/handled?`
- `How did + 人物 + feel + 情境?`

---

## 何时 / 何地

真题中「何时」仅出现 1 次、「何地」为 0 次，**不要主动出这两类题**。若确需考查时间或地点，应把时间/地点作为选项内容，而题干仍用「何事」或「何因」句式。
