# Q5 / Q6 / Q7 真题题干句式库

来源：2016–2024 年英语四级听力 News Report 全部真题，224 题逐题归类后提取。
用法：**优先套用真题原型改写**（替换人名、地名、机构名、事件），不要自造句式。

---

## 何事 · W1 事件经过型

真题原型（Q5）：
- What happened to the tour boat sailing off the Nicaraguan coast?
- What happened to Scott a month ago?
- What happened to the Philippine fisherman one day?
- What happened on the streets of Blackhall Colliery?

真题原型（Q7）：
- What happened to the little girl according to the news report?
- What has happened at Frank's restaurant since the board of free meals was created?
- What does the news report say happened finally?
- What will happen when the proposed measure comes into effect?

通用模板：
- `What happened to + 人物/事物 (+ 时间)?`
- `What has happened + since + 时间点?`
- `What does the news report say happened finally?`
- `What will happen + when + 条件?`

要点：Q7 是收尾题，W1 常以「最终结果」形态出现，答案落在原文最后一段。

---

## 何事 · W2 信息获知型

真题原型（Q5）：
- What is the news report mainly about?（多次出现）
- What do we learn about the first flight of the Airlander 10?
- What do we learn from the news report about the Japanese IT firm?
- What do we learn about the restaurant?
- What do we learn about thundersnows from the report?

真题原型（Q6）：
- What do we learn from the news report about the helicopter taxi idea?
- What do we learn from the report about the cat?

真题原型（Q7）：
- What do we learn from the news report?

通用模板：
- `What is the news report mainly about?`
- `What do we learn from the news report about + X?`
- `What do we learn about + X + from the report?`

要点：正确项是**概括或一步推断**，不是某句的直接复述。干扰项常为「原文提到但只是细节、以偏概全」。

---

## 何事 · W3 行为举措型（占比最高）

真题原型（Q5）：
- What is the U.S. Postal Service planning to do?
- What did the three university students invent?
- What have six birds been trained to do at a French historical theme park?
- What is Bangalore airport trying to do about the helicopter taxi service?
- What can people do at Frank's restaurant?
- What does the report say the village is going to do for the cat Wibur?
- What do the researchers' tests show about the cloned dog?
- What is the problem with many companies according to the news report?
- What problem is Japan facing?

真题原型（Q6）：
- What measure has been planned to save costs?
- What is Moon Express planning to do?
- What will the wildlife management company do with the captured raccoon?
- What did Steve Foran and his team do in the experiment?
- What did the restaurant manager decide to do recently?
- What did mayor Takashi Kawamura offer to do?
- What are electric bike riders supposed to do?
- What did the police do when they received the local residents' phone calls?
- What do holidaymakers come to Palmador to enjoy?
- What does Fukuda encourage his employees to do?
- What was the fisherman forced to do?

真题原型（Q7）：
- What were people advised to do when traveling outside?
- What did one online post say?
- What did the waitress do about the recovered ring?
- What is the advantage of the Airlander 10 over huge jet planes?
- What might affect Japanese domestic produce?
- What is the concern of some parents?

通用模板：
- `What did + 主体 + do/decide/attempt/offer to do?`
- `What is/are + 主体 + planning/trying/aiming to do?`
- `What were + 群体 + advised/supposed/required to do?`
- `What does the report say + 主体 + is going to do?`
- `What measure/plan + has been + planned/proposed?`
- `What do + 研究/实验 + show/find about + X?`

要点：答案是一个**动作、举措、计划或研究结论**。干扰项常为「主体错位」或「程度偏移」（计划说成已完成）。

---

## 何人（Who / 某人说了什么 / 关于某人的信息）

真题原型（Q5）：
- What does the report say about the Dutch king?
- What does Guy Wilson say his cafe aims to do?
- What does the news report say about the Japanese mayor Takashi Kawamura?

真题原型（Q7）：
- What did some Olympians say they would do with their medals?
- What does the pub owner Lesley Harper say about the cat?
- What did Rick Rahim advise parents to do with their kids?
- What do estate agents say about Palmador?
- What did the firefighters' representative tell the local TV?
- What does the king say about passengers at the airport?
- What did the policeman John Forster say about the villagers?
- Who are the targeted customers of the helicopter taxi service?

通用模板：
- `What does/did + 人物（常带职务或身份同位语）+ say (about + X)?`
- `What do we know/learn about + 人物?`
- `What did + 人物 + advise/urge/tell + 对象 + to do?`
- `Who + 谓语动词 + ...?`

要点：Q7 是收尾题，「何人」多以**引语转述**形态收尾，正确项常含 `say / think / believe` 的 paraphrase。干扰项常为「把甲的观点安给乙」。

---

## 如何（How）—— 只在 Q6 高频出现

真题原型（Q6）：
- How did Rick Rahim remove his son's loose tooth?
- How many people was the boat carrying?

真题原型（其他位置，备参考）：
- How was the woman's problem eventually solved?
- How did Kelly Swisher feel when she first came across the rat snake?
- How have Google's self-driving cars performed so far?
- How did the newly discovered creature get its name?

通用模板：
- `How did + 主体 + 动作?`（考方式、手段）
- `How many + 名词 + ...?`（考数量，注意与近音数字混淆项配合）
- `How was + 事件 + solved/handled?`
- `How did + 人物 + feel + 情境?`

要点：抽中「如何」时，原文必须明确交代**方式或数量**，否则改为抽「何事」。

---

## 何时 / 何地

真题中「何时」仅出现 1 次、「何地」为 0 次，**不要主动出这两类题**。若确需考查时间或地点，应把时间/地点作为选项内容，而题干仍用「何事」或「何人」句式。
