# Q1-Q7 真题题干句式库（精简版）

来源：2016–2024 年英语四级听力 News Report 全部真题（224 题）。
用法：套用原型改写——替换人名、地名、机构名、事件，保留句式骨架。整套生成时优先用「通用模板」，需要更贴真题时取「真题原型」。

---

## 何事 · W1 事件经过型

真题原型：
- What happened at a construction site in Brooklyn on Tuesday?（Q1）
- What happened in Scotland last Wednesday?（Q2）
- What happened to the tour boat sailing off the Nicaraguan coast?（Q5）
- What happened to the little girl according to the news report?（Q7）
- What does the news report say happened finally?（Q7）

通用模板：
- `What happened to/on/in + 地点或人物 + 时间?`
- `What finally happened to + 人物/事物?`
- `What has happened + since + 时间点?`
- `What will happen + when + 条件?`
- `What event will take place in + 地点?`

## 何事 · W2 信息获知型

真题原型：
- What is the news report mainly about?（Q1/Q3/Q5 高频）
- What do we learn about the new study from the news report?（Q1）
- What do we learn from the report about tourism in France?（Q2）
- What do we learn about the first flight of the Airlander 10?（Q5）
- What do we learn from the report about the cat?（Q6）

通用模板：
- `What is the news report mainly about?`
- `What do we learn from the news report about + X?`
- `What do we learn about + X?`
- `What is reported in the news?`

## 何事 · W3 行为举措型（占比最高）

真题原型：
- What are scientists planning to do?（Q1）
- What did Joe's parents decide to do?（Q1）
- What did the head of road policing urge drivers to do?（Q2）
- What are big fast food chains in New York City required to do according to the new rule?（Q3）
- What did government officials propose to do to protect the endangered fish?（Q4）
- What is the U.S. Postal Service planning to do?（Q5）
- What measure has been planned to save costs?（Q6）
- What were people advised to do when traveling outside?（Q7）

通用模板：
- `What did + 主体 + do/decide/attempt/offer to do?`
- `What is/are + 主体 + planning/trying/aiming to do?`
- `What were + 群体 + advised/required/urged/supposed to do?`
- `What does the news report say about + X?`
- `What measure/plan + has been + planned/proposed?`
- `What do + 研究/实验 + show/find about + X?`
- `What is the finding/problem/recommendation of + X?`

## 何因（Why / cause / purpose）

真题原型：
- Why did John Gordon move out of Abergeldie Castle?（Q1）
- What accounts most for the huge declines in tourism in France?（Q1）
- Why did Paula Pierce give Clint Buffington the reward?（Q2）
- Why were spraying operations carried out in Dorchester County?（Q3）
- What motivated the mayor of Paris to promote the first World Car-Free Day in her city?（Q4）
- Why does Japan limit the import of potatoes?（Q6）
- Why did City Council member Anna Cumber advocate the introduction of electric bikes into Jacksonville?（Q7）

通用模板：
- `Why did/does + 主体 + 动作?`
- `What is/was the cause of + 事件 (+ according to + 来源)?`
- `What caused / What has caused + 现象?`
- `What accounts for + 现象?`
- `What is the purpose of + 事物?` / `For what purpose did + 主体 + 动作?`
- `What motivated + 人物 + to + 动作?`

## 何人（Who / 引语转述 / 关于某人）

真题原型：
- What does the news report say about the waiter?（Q1）
- What does Juan Somavia, Director-General of the International Labour Organization say?（Q2）
- What do we learn about Tom Moses?（Q3）
- What did Keith Porter say at an interview?（Q4）
- What does Guy Wilson say his cafe aims to do?（Q5）
- What does the news report say about Rick Rahim?（Q6）
- What did some Olympians say they would do with their medals?（Q7）

通用模板：
- `What does/did + 人物（常带职务同位语）+ say (about + X)?`
- `What do we learn/know about + 人物 (+ from the news report)?`
- `What does the news report say about + 人物?`
- `What did + 人物 + advise/urge/tell + 对象 + to do?`
- `Who + 谓语动词 + ...?`

## 如何（How）—— 主要是 Q6

真题原型：
- How did Rick Rahim remove his son's loose tooth?（Q6）
- How many people was the boat carrying?（Q6）
- How was the woman's problem eventually solved?（Q2）
- How did Kelly Swisher feel when she first came across the rat snake?（Q1）

通用模板：
- `How did + 主体 + 动作?`
- `How many + 名词 + ...?`
- `How was + 事件 + solved/handled?`
- `How did + 人物 + feel + 情境?`

## 何时 / 何地

真题中「何时」仅 1 次、「何地」为 0 次，**不要主动出这两类题**。要考时间或地点，把它放进选项，题干仍用「何事」或「何人」句式。
